Who We Are

LUXE HAIR SALOON is a premier destination for those who seek elegance and expertise. Founded by Karen Smith, our salon blends artistry with science to deliver transformative hair experiences. We believe that every client deserves to feel confident, beautiful, and uniquely themselves.

From our Victoria Island location, we serve a discerning clientele with bespoke styling, restorative treatments, and a curated collection of luxury wigs. Our team is dedicated to continuous education and uses only the finest products.
